The problem

Facebook and Instagram ads in South Asia brought in plenty of leads. Left to a standard automated sequence, about 4% of them became eligible applicants. Interest wasn't the problem. Most students were weighing a huge decision alone, and a generic email didn't feel like anyone was on the other end.

The spell

My team built follow-up that felt like a conversation: localized for Bangladesh, Pakistan, and their neighbors, and written by people who understood each market.

The psychology is simple. A big, scary decision gets easier when someone who sounds like home is walking you through it.

What happened

From December 2024 to March 2025, more than 33% of these Meta leads became eligible applicants, compared with 4% for leads that didn't get personal follow-up. Approvals grew 2.2× in Bangladesh and 1.8× in Pakistan.

Try this at home

Before you automate a follow-up, have a person do it by hand for a week. You'll learn exactly what the automation needs to say.
