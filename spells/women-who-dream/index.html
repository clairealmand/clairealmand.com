The problem

Averages hide people. While digging through our funnel data, one pattern kept surfacing: women converted better than men at every single stage. Our marketing didn't reflect that at all. It spoke to "students" as if they were one group.

The spell

I turned the analysis into a campaign idea and partnered with Shreya Paul on our team to bring it to life. Women Who Dream spoke directly to women planning to study abroad, with stories and messages built for them rather than adapted from a generic version.

The idea had legs. We later brought it back to reach nursing students as part of a broader push to diversify where our borrowers came from.

What happened

ID-verified application submissions from women grew 2.4×, and loan volume accepted by borrowers grew 2.2×.

Try this at home

Split your conversion rates by segment before you split your budget. Your best audience may be sitting quietly inside an average.
