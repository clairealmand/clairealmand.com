Speaking and workshops

I help teams make sense of what customers believe, reach people across markets, and communicate with clarity. My talks and workshops draw on years of building growth teams and hosting conversations with international students and experts.

Formats I welcome

  • Company workshops. Hands-on sessions for marketing and leadership teams. I've built executive-communication workshops at work, and I like a room where people leave with something they'll use on Monday.
  • Conference talks and panels. A focused talk, or a seat on a panel where the questions get real.
  • Podcasts and interviews. Honest conversations about building a career, a team, or a life in a new country.
  • Hosting and moderation. Live Q&As, webinars, and panel conversations, keeping the experts clear and the audience in the conversation.

What I talk about

  • Customer psychology and trust. Why people believe you, or don't, and what to do when your best prospects think you might be a scam. (That one really happened.)
  • International growth. Reaching customers who are deciding from the other side of the world, from South Asia to a first event in Nepal.
  • Leadership and executive communication. Growing a team from one part-time MBA student to 10 people, and making the case to the people who sign off.

Long bio

208 words

Claire Almand reads people for a living, then builds marketing that meets them where they are. She is the co-founder and CEO of Rarecraft, a senior marketing team that learns why customers buy, then ships the positioning, campaigns, and AI workflows that change the answer. She also works with companies as a CMO for a season.

Claire spent a decade at MPOWER Financing, helping international students pay for their education, and rose from MBA intern to Director of Growth Marketing. She grew the digital team from two people to 10, led the relaunch that tripled qualified leads after a year-long pandemic shutdown, and opened Nepal as a new market, explaining it herself on Kantipur TV, Radio Kantipur, and NTV World. Under her leadership, loan applications and disbursed loan volume grew about 23×.

Before MPOWER, she ran marketing for a restaurant and gallery and built websites for small businesses through her own consultancy. She holds a B.A. in Psychology from Swarthmore College and an MBA from the University of Maryland's Robert H. Smith School of Business, and won a Built In Moxie Award for Women in Tech in 2023. She writes the Growth Grimoire, a newsletter on the psychology behind growth, and is usually up early photographing the sunrise.

Videos are from the Yudi J, MPOWER Financing, Kantipur, and NTV World YouTube channels, shared as examples of my speaking, not as current advice.
