The scenic route

Fifteen years, the long way around.

Psychology, a restaurant, a wizard consultancy, an MBA, a decade of growth marketing, and a company of my own. It looks random from a distance. Up close it's one question asked over and over: why do people say yes?

Follow a thread
2011 to 2013 · Reading people
  1. 2011

    Swarthmore College

    B.A. in Psychology, with minors in Biology and English Literature. Brains, bodies, and stories: still the whole toolkit.

  2. 2011 to 2013

    Psychology analyst

    Tryon Therapy and The Child & Family Center. I turned behavioral data and client records into recommendations for intervention plans, therapy, and legal cases. Data about people, used to help people, with real stakes.

2012 to 2016 · The Eastern Shore
  1. 2012 to 2015

    The Globe

    A restaurant with a gallery upstairs. As administrator and marketing manager, I grew our social and email audiences 4×, launched a loyalty program, modernized reservations, and led a five-person team. Restaurants teach you customer psychology at full speed.

  2. 2014 to 2016

    Professional Wizardry

    My own consultancy. I built 10 websites plus the branding, social, email, and measurement behind them for small businesses and nonprofits, then trained the owners to run it all without me.

  3. 2015 to 2016

    Worcester County tourism

    Social media for the county, mostly with my own sunrise photos and video. Followers tripled, from 5,000 to 15,000. The camera has been in my bag ever since.

2016 to 2018 · Back to school
  1. 2016 to 2018

    MBA, University of Maryland

    Robert H. Smith School of Business, concentrating in marketing, innovation, and entrepreneurship. As a graduate assistant, I helped launch the school's first executive communication program and led six workshops in its second year.

  2. 2017

    A laugh at a career fair

    A carful of international friends, loud dance music, and a startup career fair in DC. MPOWER Financing heard my laugh and offered me a marketing internship.

2018 to 2021 · MPOWER, the foundation years
  1. 2018 to 2019

    Associate Manager, Marketing

    Digital marketing was two people. I took on more of the channels, the data, and the questions nobody else had time for.

  2. 2019 to 2021

    Manager, Marketing

    Expanded acquisition across South Asia, Sub-Saharan Africa, China, and Latin America. Converted contractors to employees and laid the foundation for a team with two layers of management.

2021 to 2023 · The comeback
  1. May 2021 to Oct 2022

    Senior Manager: restarting the engine

    After a year-long pandemic shutdown, I planned and led the relaunch. Qualified leads tripled from pre-pandemic levels within six months. I also hired Sagar Somani, now my Rarecraft co-founder.

    Read the spell
  2. 2021 to 2022

    “Is this a scam?”

    Trustpilot and live Q&As for students meeting a new brand for the first time. I hosted more than 90% of the first 25+ sessions; late-funnel conversions grew 4×.

    Read the spell
  3. Oct 2022 to Nov 2025

    Associate Director: same budget, more volume

    Promoted to Associate Director. In 2023, on a flat budget, revenue grew 1.8× and qualified leads 2.3×. A Built In Moxie Award for Women in Tech followed.

2022 to 2025 · Growing up, not just out
  1. 2022 to 2025

    Breaking the paid-ads habit

    Shifted investment to organic search, referrals, and student ambassadors as each matured. Customer acquisition cost fell 90%.

    Read the spell
  2. 2022 to 2025

    Building the India team

    By 2023, our India digital team had reached 42% female representation, and I had mentored 8+ women through coaching and stretch assignments. I also spent five and a half months onsite in India on a leadership rotation.

  3. 2025

    Women Who Dream

    The data showed women converted better at every stage of the funnel. I spotted the opportunity and partnered with Shreya Paul to launch a campaign for them. Qualified leads from women grew 2.4×.

    Read the spell
  4. 2024 to 2025

    The human touch

    Our team's localized follow-up in South Asia turned 33%+ of social media leads into qualified applicants, against 4% without personal follow-up.

    Read the spell
2025 to 2026 · Director
  1. Nov 2025 to Jul 2026

    Director, Growth Marketing

    Rebuilt paid advertising in-house with sharper targeting, measurement, and creative, and brought AI into the team's daily work. One recurring report went from an hour to five minutes.

  2. 2025 to 2026

    Bina dhito: opening Nepal

    I launched MPOWER in Nepal around one message, no collateral, and explained it myself on Kantipur TV, Radio Kantipur, and NTV World. Our first event drew 1,500 qualified prospects and turned away 1,500 more.

    Read the spell
  3. 2017 to 2026

    The whole MPOWER arc

    Promoted five times, from MBA intern to Director. Digital marketing grew from two people to 10 staff, six agencies, and 50+ contractors, and our team grew loan applications and disbursed loan volume about 23× (April to December, 2018 compared with 2025).

2026 · Something new is brewing
  1. 2026

    Rarecraft

    Co-founder and CEO, with Sagar Somani and Aakash Sethi. A senior marketing team that learns why customers buy, then ships the positioning, campaigns, and AI workflows that change the answer.
