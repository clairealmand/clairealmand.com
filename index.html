A CMO, for a season.

Some people call this fractional. I call it a CMO for a season: someone senior who can read the room, make the calls, and leave the team stronger than she found it. Some companies don't need a whole marketing department yet. They need that.

Been there: MPOWER's growth team went from one part-time MBA student to 10 people across the US and India.

Growth unstuck

Been there: 1.8× funded loan volume on a flat budget in 2023, and 3× qualified leads six months after a lending pause.

How working together starts

  1. A 30-minute conversation. You tell me where growth is stuck. I ask a lot of questions about your customers. Book it here.
  2. I read the room. Before I recommend anything, I look at what your customers believe, what your data says, and what your team is already carrying.
  3. We pick the shape. Part-time and ongoing, or interim, which means stepping in for a defined transition or leadership gap. Either way it runs through Rarecraft, so the team is there when you need hands as well as a head.

I take CMO roles through Rarecraft, the firm I co-founded. Start with one senior leader inside your team, and bring in the full team when the work calls for it.
